02What is obsession

ob·ses·sion / noun

An idea or thought that continually preoccupies a person's mind. At first you hold the thought. Then the thought holds you.

LOSS OF CONTROL
Obsession is not a feeling. It is a machine — five parts, running in order, on everyone it touches.
Notice the cycle.
CRUSH — Bear, for years, quietly.
ATTENTION — watching her across every room.
REPETITION — re-reading, re-checking, re-wanting.
FIXATION — the wish. One sentence, no edits.
LOSS OF CONTROL — the rest of the film.

09The back half of the film
WATCHING, AS LOVE

The back half of the movie is mostly Nikki looking at Bear. At the party, the obsession turns outward — and Sarah pays for standing between them.

Did you notice that?
She is in nearly every frame of the second act — usually at the edge, usually still.
Keep your cursor moving. It dislikes stillness.
10One more time
ONE MORE WISH.
Ian's exit: a billion dollars, no strings, dead anyway. The charm is neutral. People aren't.
Bear keeps the wish even after he understands it. He doesn't want it fixed — he wants it to work.
